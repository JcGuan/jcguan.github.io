Dr Jingcheng Guan

Computational chemistry, computational physics, materials chemistry, and scientific computing.

About Me

I am an experienced computational scientist specialising in multiscale materials modelling, with a strong track record of high-impact research published in leading scientific journals. At the heart of my work is a passion for mathematics, which I regard not only as a rigorous scientific tool but also as a source of creativity and discovery. Building on this passion since my student years, I bring together mathematics, computation, and materials science to develop innovative approaches to fundamental scientific challenges and contribute to a more sustainable future. My research focuses on hybrid QM/MM embedding techniques, vibrational spectroscopy, and scientific computing.

Research

I am interested in interacting phonons and local vibrations, anharmonic spectroscopy, PES representation, and machine-learning approaches for molecular simulations, physics of defects in materials, and catalysis.
